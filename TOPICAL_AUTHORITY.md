# Topical Authority — gadget.codev.id

## Role and boundary

`gadget.codev.id` is a practical Indonesian guide and commercial-support property for people choosing, inspecting, buying, setting up, caring for, and reselling used phones and laptops. Educational pages own durable decision criteria and testing workflows; existing `/gadget/jual-...-bekas` routes own model-level stock, specifications, price, and conversion. Product availability, specifications, prices, warranty terms, device/security status, privacy procedures, and current software behavior must be verified at publication and transaction time.

## Context used

- Homepage title and H1 identify the offer as affordable used phones and laptops.
- Visible promises emphasize affordability, safe payment, fast process, customer service, and a product catalog.
- Navigation exposes product hubs for Laptop and Handphone plus brand routes including Asus, Acer, Lenovo, HP, MacBook, Dell, Samsung, Xiaomi, Apple, Vivo, Oppo, Poco, Infinix, and Realme.
- Sitemaps contain 1,803 URLs: 30 page routes, 662 model-level `/gadget/jual-...-bekas` commercial pages, 265 `/wilayah/` location templates, 837 `wp-json` technical URLs, 2 category archives, and 6 demo/quote posts.
- The 662 model pages are a substantial commercial catalog but not an editorial article corpus. The six post URLs are generic demo/quote content, so title-only planning does not require deep mode when proposed articles remain model-agnostic.

## Ignored template noise

- The 265 location-swapped sales pages were counted as one geographic template family; their bodies and metadata were not analyzed.
- The 662 model-level product routes were counted as one commercial family; their current stock, specifications, and prices were not treated as evergreen editorial facts.
- `wp-json`, feeds, comments, category archives, WordPress/framework files, media assets, and pagination were excluded from editorial evidence.
- Six generic demo/quote posts were treated as contamination rather than topical coverage.
- Brand labels identify inventory/navigation families, not permission to create thin model-by-model review articles.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel mengarah ke hub topiknya. GDC-01 membagi pembaca ke GDC-02 atau GDC-03; keduanya menaut ke pemeriksaan GDC-04 sampai GDC-11. GDC-12 dan GDC-13 mengubah hasil pemeriksaan menjadi keputusan transaksi, sedangkan GDC-14 menjadi checklist pengikat. Sesudah pembelian, pembaca bergerak ke GDC-15 lalu GDC-16. Rute produk hanya menerima tautan ketika pembaca telah menetapkan kebutuhan dan kriteria inspeksi; artikel tidak mengulang halaman model, stok, spesifikasi, atau harga.
